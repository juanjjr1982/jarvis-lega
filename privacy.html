JARVIS – Privacy Policy

JARVIS is a private, single-user app operated by its owner for personal use only.

JARVIS only reads statistics of the owner's own TikTok account: profile name, follower count, and video views, likes, comments and shares. It never posts, changes or deletes content.

The data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, never shared with third parties and never used for advertising.

The owner can disconnect JARVIS at any time in the TikTok app settings, which stops all access.
